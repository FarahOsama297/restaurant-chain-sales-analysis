# 🍽️ Restaurant Chain Sales Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end sales analysis project for a restaurant chain, designed to evaluate business performance, monitor sales, understand customer behavior, and identify opportunities for revenue growth.

The project uses Power BI to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Analyze customer and order activity.
- Evaluate branch and cashier performance.
- Identify geographical sales trends.
- Understand sales distribution by order type and payment method.
- Support business decisions through actionable insights.

## 🧹 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Reviewed data quality and checked data consistency.
- Handled invalid values and prepared the dataset for analysis.
- Transformed and organized data using Power Query.

## 📊 2. Data Analysis

Performed analysis across multiple business dimensions:

- Total sales, number of orders, and customers.
- Branch and cashier performance.
- Geographical sales distribution.
- Order types and payment methods.
- Daily sales trends and performance changes.

## 📈 3. Interactive Power BI Dashboard

Developed an interactive dashboard featuring:

- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- Branch and cashier performance analysis.
- Geographical sales distribution.
- Sales by order type and payment method.
- Daily sales performance.
- Interactive filters for dynamic data exploration.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Total Sales:** Approximately SAR 2.64 million.
- **Total Orders:** 6,623.
- **Customers:** Approximately 5,000.
- **Top Region:** Region 2, contributing 44.37% of total sales.
- **Top Branch:** Al Aqiq branch.
- **Order Type:** Delivery orders generated the highest revenue contribution.
- **Payment Method:** Cash payments exceeded deferred payments in total sales.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

1. Leverage the practices of top-performing branches.
2. Improve sales performance in lower-contributing regions.
3. Enhance delivery services to strengthen a major revenue channel.
4. Improve sales team performance by learning from top-performing employees.
5. Encourage the adoption of electronic payment methods.
6. Investigate sales growth drivers to support future marketing campaigns.

Transformed sales data into actionable business insights to help management evaluate performance, identify growth opportunities, and make more informed business decisions.
